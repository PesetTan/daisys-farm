# Daisy completion artwork

Created with the built-in image-generation tool, using the approved autumn Daisy portrait as the identity and painting reference. Outputs: `assets/daisy-happy-{spring,summer,autumn,winter}.png` and `assets/daisy-tail.png`.

## Portrait prompt

Use case: identity-preserve. Transparent game mascot sprite for Daisy’s Farm, match this exact hound-beagle Daisy and Windswept gouache painting. Change her head to face directly at the viewer with soft happy eye contact, relaxed friendly mouth with just a small pink tongue tip showing. Retain long floppy tan ears, white muzzle and chest, black saddle, same proportions and seated body. Keep her paws hidden in tall windswept grass. IMPORTANT no visible tail: it is a separate animated layer behind this sprite. Composition dog centered with head in upper center, rump at 55% across and 70% down, grass clustered across lower half. Entire silhouette with small transparent margins, square canvas. No text or background. Broad painterly strokes, not photoreal. Seasonal palette: 

- spring: fresh lime and lush green grass, soft clear spring light
- summer: dry golden tawny grass and warm summer sunlight
- autumn: olive ochre russet grass, mellow autumn light
- winter: rain green and muted tan grass, cool blue-gray winter light, no snow

## Corrected tail prompt

Reference Daisy’s actual photos IMG_1523 and IMG_0417 for shape and markings, and the autumn portrait for painting style. Render only a short-coated tapering hound tail: charcoal-black upper surface from root nearly to tip, tan underside, dark brown end. No white tip, plume, halo or fluffy fur. Slim, gently curved diagonal with root bottom right and tip top left. Broad matte Windswept gouache, transparent background. Supersedes the rejected golden-and-white generic tail.
